How to Play

  • Conquest. Stand inside a flag's circle to capture it. Each flag you own scores a point every second, a capture gives a bonus and every kill adds one. First to 500, or the higher score when time runs out, wins.
  • Team Deathmatch. Eliminations are the only score. Reach the target before the other army does.
  • Free for All. No armies: every fighter is on their own and spawns at a random spot. Most kills when the timer runs out wins.
  • Fighting. Swings take a moment to land. Block with the right mouse button while facing your enemy to cut damage sharply, but every blocked hit drains stamina. If it runs out your guard breaks.
  • Weapons. The sword is balanced, the war axe hits hard and cleaves several enemies, the spear reaches farthest with a narrow thrust. The longbow shoots arrows: hold the left mouse button to draw, release to loose. A longer draw flies faster and hits harder, and headshots do extra damage. Arrows land where your crosshair points, but moving targets need a lead. Hold the right button to zoom in. Shields and walls stop arrows.
  • Towers and walls. Every tower has a door at the bottom. Walk inside, face the ladder, hold W to climb, and use the floors, windows and roof to shoot from height. The big towers have three floors. The second floor of each tower opens onto the wall walk, so you can climb up and walk along the top of the castle walls, through the gatehouses and over the gates. Parapets stop you walking off, but jumping off a high wall hurts.
  • Fortress Siege. Attackers (Blue) win by killing the king inside the keep; defenders (Red) win if he lives until the timer ends. Break the gate with the battering ram (press E behind it, more crew means faster), or climb a wall ladder and fight along the battlements. Defenders hold the walls, ballistas and keep slits, and fall back to the keep when overrun. Horses (E) are fast and can be hurt or killed, but cannot climb. Defenders spawn on the keep roof and run down the ramp. Catapults at the front line (E to man one, or E to take it from your own crewman; hold click to wind the arm back, release to fling the stone; hold R beside one to drag it slowly) smash the gate and hit defenders on the walls.
  • Friendly fire. It is on for everyone. Swings and arrows hit teammates in the way, and enemy soldiers hit theirs too, so watch your swing in a crowd and keep allies out of your line of fire. Killing a teammate scores nothing.
  • Towers. Walk in through the door, face the ladder and hold W to climb. Archers on both sides will climb the towers too and shoot from the top, so expect arrows from above.
  • Allies. Your soldiers push to flags, defend what you hold and help each other. Fight beside them, because a lone knight falls quickly.
  • Minimap. Flags show who owns them. Blue and red dots are soldiers. The white arrow is you.
